Key Capabilities

Designed for Accuracy, Speed & Clinical Precision

Everything required to run clinical laboratory testing smoothly—from specimen collection and automated hematology calculations to doctor-signed printable A4 outputs.

🔬

Automated Clinical Calculators

Eliminates manual hematology math. Automatically computes MCV, MCH, MCHC, and PCV from RBC count and hemoglobin in real time with abnormal boundary detection.

🖨️

Master A4 Clinical Reports

Pixel-perfect international A4 standard printing with Dr. Shahid Khan (MBBS) authorized signature block, custom barcodes, and direct client-side PDF export.

📦

Smart Consumables Tracking

Automatic decrement tracking of purple EDTA tubes, red plain tubes, Typhoid kits, and Malaria rapid test kits with PIN-protected inventory reconciliation.

💰

Financial & Revenue Auditing

Accurate segregation of Cash vs. Online payments, real-time daily totals, PIN-gated privacy views, and integrated patient diagnostic bill & invoice generation.

⚡

Dual-Mode Offline & Cloud

Runs completely offline via local browser storage (`file:///` protocol) and synchronizes seamlessly in the background with Supabase PostgreSQL cloud database.

📂

Patient Archives & Deep Search

Instantly search, retrieve, and filter historical diagnostic records by Patient ID, Name, Contact Number, or Date Range with one-click preview and editing.
